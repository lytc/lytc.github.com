Legacy modernization

I advocated retiring or replacing legacy PHP and Ruby applications and helped plan those transitions. The work included proposing newer application architectures and moving existing functionality toward newer services.

The transitions connected existing behavior to newer services, including file-management and notification functionality. Legacy modernization has been an ongoing effort rather than a single rewrite.

Repository consolidation

I directly implemented frontend monorepo changes in 2020 and helped introduce the shared workspace to the team. Later, I helped drive the discussion and adoption of a backend monorepo in 2024, contributed service and infrastructure migrations, and worked on the local development setup.

The backend transition was shared work. Another engineer built the initial proof of concept; I contributed to the direction, evaluation, migration, and team guidance.

Serverless adoption

I championed using serverless execution for suitable workloads, helped evaluate limitations and infrastructure choices, and contributed to implementation and operational integration. That included work on observability for Lambda-based execution and later service migrations.

The discussions involved real tradeoffs: migration effort, platform constraints, deployment practices, and what the team could maintain. My role included making the proposal concrete enough for others to evaluate and adopt.

Delivered change, measured carefully

The delivered outcomes include shared repository and cloud tooling, service migrations, and development guidance for adoption. These establish concrete changes in how the systems are organized and delivered. Productivity gains and cost savings are not quantified here.
