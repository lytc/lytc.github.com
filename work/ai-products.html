The product

Digication lets educators configure AI assistants for assignments and reflection. OAIR is its public hub for educational AI resources. My contribution spans the interfaces, APIs, and shared code that connect those experiences.

What I built and contributed

I contributed early AI assistant interfaces, work connecting OAIR assistants to Digication, and subsequent frontend and API changes. This includes assistant interactions, typed data access, and integration with the surrounding learning product.

I also built out a shared AI backend package by extracting and extending common assistant capabilities. The work includes shared representations of assistants, threads, messages, and runs, so related applications can use a common foundation.

The engineering shape

The stack combines TypeScript, React, GraphQL, and Node.js with model-provider SDKs. My work crosses product UI, service behavior, and the reusable layer beneath them.

The important engineering task is connecting model output to a usable product: maintaining conversation state, fitting the surrounding workflow, and handling the application behavior beyond a single model request.

My contribution in context

This is shared product development, with my role in implementation and integration. Educational design, product direction, and other engineering work also come from colleagues. I do not claim authorship of the pedagogy or a measured improvement in learning outcomes.

Much of my recent implementation work is AI-assisted. The technical decisions, review, and responsibility for my submitted changes remain with me.
