03 / HOW I WORK

Agents do work.
People own decisions.

Much of my recent implementation is AI-assisted. I stay responsible for the choices, the checks, and the changes I submit.

?

Start with what needs to be true.

Understand the behavior being changed, the surrounding system, and the constraints. A clear problem gives both the implementation and its review something to be tested against.

Intent → constraints → a checkable change
⌘

Use the agent. Keep the context.

I use coding agents for much of the implementation work. The existing code, its conventions, and the actual requirements still matter. I inspect what changed rather than treating an agent’s explanation as the finished work.

Implementation is an input to review.
◇

Give the change another point of view.

Another model challenges the proposed change. Risk guides the review effort, and disagreements lead back to the diff, the requirements, and relevant checks. A second model adds scrutiny; it does not certify correctness.

A finding needs a reason you can inspect.
↗

Keep a person at the decision point.

I inspect diffs, run relevant checks, and decide what to accept. Consequential actions keep explicit human approval. I own the changes I submit, including those implemented with an agent.

Evidence → judgment → responsibility
