DELIVERY & IMPACT

Separate different kinds of result

An implemented feature, a completed migration, and colleague use are concrete outcomes. They do not by themselves establish a productivity gain, cost saving, or defect reduction.

The portfolio does not attach unmeasured percentages to internal work. Recent implementation is often AI-assisted, with responsibility for my submitted changes retained by me.

PUBLIC SCOPE

Explain the work without exposing it

Company work is described in generalized form. Internal source code, private messages, student or customer data, and operational metrics are not reproduced. Explanatory diagrams are original, not screenshots of internal systems.

Security and compliance contributions refer to engineering support and evidence tooling, not a claim of certification or audit completion. Readers can inspect public product context, but not the private attribution records here.
